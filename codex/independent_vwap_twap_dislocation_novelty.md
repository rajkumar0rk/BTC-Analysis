# Novelty gate — hourly VWAP–TWAP dislocation

Hypothesis: within a completed hour, the volume-weighted average traded typical price can deviate from the equal-time average when market participation is concentrated at different prices. The sign and magnitude of that deviation might indicate a temporary inventory-transfer/price-impact state. OHLCV cannot identify aggressive buyer/seller flow or prove an institutional cause. Test both continuation and reversal; compare with zero-signal baseline and prior volume-backload research before interpreting.

Distinct from `analysis/independent_hourly_volume_backload_v1/` (last-quarter volume share regardless of execution-price level), `analysis/institutional_discovery_stage19_monthly_10pct/` (candle shock/RSI), and VWAP reclaim strategies (close crossing daily VWAP). This experiment compares **hourly volume-weighted typical price to hourly equal-time typical price** and thresholds their difference by completed hourly ATR; it does not use daily VWAP crosses, a last-quarter share or a candle-direction signal. No ETH-driven parameter selection.

BTC 2025 chooses a single shared threshold and continuation/reversal direction; freeze it for BTC 2026 and all ETH results. Native SHA-validated 1m, 22 IST months, 0.5% equity risk/trade, max 3x notional, 1.5 ATR stop, 2R target, 12h timeout, stop-first 1m replay, 0.05% taker fee each side plus 18% GST, BTC $5/side slip, ETH 0.02%/side slip. Funding, actual spread/impact, margin/liquidation, lot sizes and live fills remain unmodeled. Selection on research-exposed 2025, later 2026 also previously seen.
