## Hypothesis and predeclared method
At the moment of a fully closed 15-minute signal, use only **completed 1-hour EMA20/EMA50 and ADX14** to classify the prevailing BTCUSD market into TREND_UP, TREND_DOWN, RANGE, TRANSITION. Test objective price-action event families from already-observed prior candle ranges, volatility, RSI and relative volume, including breakout confirmation, EMA20 pullback, squeeze ignition, inside-bar breakout, failed breakout sweep and Bollinger reentry. Evaluate two *predeclared* volume-strength regimes (plain, high-volume) and market-state requirements, without picking a regime in hindsight.

**Entry and exit:** Delta India BTCUSD perpetual native 1m archive; completed 15m setup enters **next actual 1m OPEN**. One opening per complete IST calendar date per candidate, first signal of the day, long or short, seven days/week. Simulate **gross chart-price +200 BTC TP and −100 BTC SL**, stop takes priority if same minute hits both. Gap stop at worse minute open, favorable TP never improves fill. If no TP/SL, close at 240-minute timeout or final minute of IST date. 1× notional of current account NAV, starting US$10,000. Net P&L subtracts Delta assumed 0.05% taker fee each side plus 18% GST, plus 5 BTC chart-points adverse slippage each side. Funding, bid-ask, impact, contract step, liquidations not modeled.

**Causal market-state detection:** ADX14 uses completed 1H high/low/close and Wilder smoothing, EMA20/EMA50 completed hour, 1H true range and 15m Bollinger/RSI are completed only. TREND_UP/DOWN when ADX≥25, EMA20 above/below EMA50 and hourly close on trend side; RANGE when ADX≤18; remaining bars TRANSITION. Additional hours are unclassified until a full rolling 1H lookback is available. NO signal uses intra-1h current candle or future price. Signal families are deterministic and compared separately, along with fixed regime masks; a conservative no-trade threshold is allowed (and encouraged).

**Selection:** Pre-register candidate collection and all parameters in script; **2025 training only** ranks viable setups by 2025 mean after-cost BTC-point return with conservative confidence score and **min 30 independent trading days in each 2025 half**, positive mean in *both* halves and positive estimated 95%-style lower mean bound. January–June 2026 and July–October 2026 are **unchanged later checks**: date windows were researched in prior project, so NOT pristine unseen OOS. Do not optimize after observing their returns. If NO 2025 candidate passes these tests, report NO QUALIFYING STRATEGY, but still show top-2025 candidates and later performance as research diagnostics; do not present them as deployment recommendations. Up to one candidate per IST day, first event, no synthetic portfolio of separately tested candidates. Negative-day and no-trade-day counts mandatory.

**Evaluation:** 2025 H1, 2025 H2, Jan–Jun 2026, Jul–Oct 2026, all monthly returns, starting/final NAV, win/TP first percentages, exit-reason counts, fee/GST/slippage, profit factor, max closed-trade drawdown, gross vs net-equivalent BTC points, 2025 rank not conditional on future splits, candidate table and rejected candidates. Explicit day boundary caveat: first archive minute Jan 1 2025 00:00 UTC means Jan1 IST partial; final archive Oct30 2026 23:59 UTC means Oct31 IST partial. Only Jan2 2025–Oct30 2026 IST dates included. October 2026 is NOT full archive month.
