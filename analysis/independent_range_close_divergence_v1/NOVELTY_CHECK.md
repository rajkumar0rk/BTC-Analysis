# Novelty assessment: Range-vs-close variance divergence
Decision: NEW, subject to explicit comparison with previous volatility and candle-efficiency studies.

Economic hypothesis: When intrahour price excursions are large relative to net close-to-close price discovery, liquidity replenishment may dominate and the next 12h return may reverse the latest hourly direction. The rival hypothesis is continuation as trapped positions unwind. This is an OHLC-observable **proxy**, NOT evidence of actual liquidity, inventory, forced flow, or order-book absorption.

At each completed hour, compute six-hour summed squared log(high/low) divided by six-hour summed squared log(close/previous close), with minimum denominator. Require ratio >= 3 or >= 5 and last hourly absolute log return >= 0.5 or >=1.0 times its trailing 24h RMS. Enter opposite the last completed hour at the next 1m open. Exits: ATR(14) of completed hourly bars x1.5 stop, 2R profit, 12h timeout; 1m stop-first gap-aware; 0.5% account risk, 3x notional cap; no overlapping trades. Same normalized rules BTC and ETH. 2025-only shared selection, unchanged 2026 check. Full 22-month IST NAV and exit-PF audit.
