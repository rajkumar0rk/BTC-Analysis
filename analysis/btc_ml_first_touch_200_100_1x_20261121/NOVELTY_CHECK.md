# Novelty gate — supervised first-touch probability at BTC +200/−100, 1× NAV (2026-11-21)

**Decision: PARTIAL OVERLAP — approved material MODEL difference, NOT automatically a new edge.**

## Problem
The user requests one BTCUSD futures scalp per day if justified, aiming at a +200 BTC price-point take profit and −100 BTC price-point stop on 1× equity. Historical deterministic indicator searches have returned losses. We must determine whether using *learned conditional probability* of +200 before −100 can identify a **sufficiently small reliable favorable subset**, based solely on data known at order time. Never imply guaranteed daily profitability or artificially force 1 trade/day.

## Exact, predeclared protocol — NO 2026 tuning

Data: original Delta India BTCUSD perpetual 1m OHLCV, 2025-Jan–Oct-2026 (archive only through 2026-Oct30 UTC). Candidate event: each fully complete 15-minute candle ending at 15-minute boundary; enter at exactly next 1-minute OPEN. Compute only past and just-closed 15m/1h candles, 15m EMA, ATR, RSI, Bollinger z-score, VWAP-proxy, past relative volume, hourly EMA20/EMA50, hourly ADX, 1h/4h return and ATR relative, consecutive candles, candle strength/range, day-of-week/time-of-day cyclic features. Do not use future 1m prices for features or any incomplete hourly OHLC. Build two row labels (candidate LONG and SHORT) with first-path outcome at raw chart-price +200 versus −100; 1m stop-first on intraminute simultaneous touches, gap stop worse than threshold, max 240 1m bars or IST date end; no cross-midnight positions. Separately record actual realized after-cost net BTC-equivalent point payoffs. Price-level thresholds are chart gross BTC points, not after-cost net points.

Economics: initial $10,000 simulated equity, position size 1× CURRENT NAV in BTC-equivalent, execution adverse 5 BTC points EACH SIDE, 0.05% taker entry and exit +18% GST each, time exits at 1m close. Funding, actual bid/ask, L2, book depth, mark liquidation, contractual lot rounding excluded. Stop hit first if both within 1m candle.

**Model**: scikit-learn `HistGradientBoostingClassifier`, one predeclared model with fixed hyperparameters, trained on 2025-Jan02–2025-Jun30 only. Target positive class: 200 chart point TP first (even if chart TP after fees may still lose at high BTC). Include both candidate sides as independent rows but their date groups overlap, so no ordinary row-level random split. Inference only on 2025-Jul–Dec and 2026 data. Pinned feature list and model hyperparameters before any run. Use 2025-Jul–Dec as **calibration/selection** to decide whether to trade at all and pick ONE probability gate from `[0.55,0.60,0.65,0.70,0.75,0.80,0.85]`, with predicted probability threshold plus independently predeclared fee-aware threshold based on BTC price and TP/SL expected net return. No thresholds chosen based on 2026 results. Stop training labels at last full IST day per partition, with gap embargo in training end (at least 240 minutes). Rank/calibrate using 2025 second half: min 40 independent trades and >=4 net-positive months of its 6, positive compounded net account return, net PF>1, vs no-trade default. If no gate qualifies, **NO STRATEGY**, but still run/report a 2025-selected *exploratory benchmark* clearly marked NOT QUALIFIED. Avoid re-tuning after seeing validation.

Test final selected unchanged on 2026-Jan–Jun and 2026-Jul–Oct (all previously researched, NOT untouched universe-level OOS); report 2025-H2, 2026-H1, 2026-H2 independently, 2025 Jan-Jun training is NOT an equity evaluation period; do not compound training fits into observed NAV. Only one position/day per policy. True 1m replay for selected events, compare actual net USD, realized NAV, 200-first counts, 100-first counts, exits by timeout, days with positive/negative/no-trade, 2026 10-month monthly returns and quality.
