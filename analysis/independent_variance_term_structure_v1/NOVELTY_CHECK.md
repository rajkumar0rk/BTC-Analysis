# Novelty assessment — realized-volatility term structure, 2026-11-19

Hypothesis: an unusual concentration of variance over the last four completed hours, relative to the last 48 completed hours, with coherent signed returns, predicts continued price discovery over the next 12 hours. This tests **volatility term-structure expansion with directional persistence**, not isolated jump variation, an RSI crossover, funding carry, or a raw breakout. It does not establish liquidation or dealer inventory pressure: those would require independent flow data.

Signal at completed 1h boundary: RMS(log hourly returns,4) / RMS(log hourly returns,48) >= 1.4 or 1.8; abs(sum(last four hourly returns))/sum(abs(last four hourly returns)) >= 0.65 or 0.85. Trade with the sign of the four-hour net return at next native 1m open. Stops 1.5x completed-hour ATR14, take profit 2R, 12h time exit, stop-first on 1m, 0.5% equity risk, 3x notional cap, no overlapping positions.
