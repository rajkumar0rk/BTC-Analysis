# BTC Four-Layer Past-Only Edge Selector — Research Novelty Gate (2026-11-21)

**Decision: PARTIAL OVERLAP — approved differential diagnostic, not a new strategy alpha family.**

The user requests improvement after the completed 4-layer BTC 1H/4H regime -> eligibility -> 15M confirmation -> 1M execution strategy lost -22.1561% across 22 complete IST calendar months and earned 9/22 positive months. The problem was not source data integrity or candle lookahead; the existing eligible trend, range and up-breakout groups had poor net expectancy. Do NOT hard-code the historically successful groups, as that would leak knowledge from the 2025-2026 test period into choices. The proposed change: **a strictly as-of, trailing-month, cost-aware strategy/regime eligibility gate**, using ONLY trade results from earlier fully completed months, and an independent new full 1M account replay.

## Fixed design before this run

- No changes to original `four_layer.context(one)` and `four_layer.execute(one,hour,events,model)`: 1H/last-completed 4H current state -> fixed eligible strategy -> first subsequent completed 15M directional confirmation -> native 1M next-open entry -> 1M stop-first initial stop/target/timeout, IST month-end flatten, single globally exclusive position.
- Same frozen models and costs: trend pullback 1.6 ATR stop, 2.5R TP, 24h maximum; shock breakout and structural breakout 1.8 ATR stop, 3R TP, 36h maximum; range BB fade 1.2 ATR stop, 1.5R TP, 12h maximum. Start hypothetical $10,000, risk 0.5% of changing equity per trade capped by 3× notional. Taker fee 0.05% each side plus GST 18% (0.00059) and adverse 5 BTCUSD points each side, no funding cashflows, true historical spread/orderbook, liquidation, queue, or exchange contract rounding. Native 1M SHA256 verified 992,160 consecutive minutes, 22 full IST months Jan 2025 through Oct 2026.
- Training source: independently reproduce the entire previously registered 4-layer 15M-confirmed **shadow account** trade ledger. Verify identical count 337 and -22.1561% net before any new replay; training records are completed **shadow trades**, not the new gated account outcomes. This prevents the strategy from forgetting untraded opportunities. Because baseline has exclusive positions, its shadow trades are a *subset* of all candidate events, and may be selected by time availability; explicitly note survivorship/selection caveat.
- Selector A (fixed primary): at each IST calendar month's beginning from **July 2025**, for each exact pair `(strategy,state)`, use ONLY the preceding **6 complete IST calendar months** of shadow trades with actual exit **strictly earlier** than current month open. Require at least **8 completed shadow trades** spanning **3 different training months**, sum of realized **net R > 0**, and after-cost profit factor (sum winning net R / magnitude of negative net R) **>= 1.10**. Also require total past 3 full IST months net R > 0; zero/no evidence means **NO TRADE**. Qualified pairs retain the exact original signal and 15M confirmation. No additional universal trade/day limit. Month Jan–Jun 2025 is exclusively warmup; those six 0-return months count as nonprofitable in 22/22 metric.
- Selector B (predeclared robustness): require same tests over preceding **12 complete IST months**, at least **16 completed shadow trades** across **5 different months**, PF >=1.10, net R sum >0 and most recent 3 complete months sum net R >0; first 12 months are training-only. This is a robustness check, not an a-posteriori best-choice optimization; do not promote B if it merely looks better on seen 2026.
- Baselines: `no_trade` and fully ungated `four_layer_15m_confirmed`, independently replayed and checked for exact parity. All comparators have separate exclusive position/account and identical execution constraints.
- Decision CSV: month, strategy, state, training dates, training sample count, active training months, net-R PF, trailing3 net-R, eligibility decision and explicit failure reason. Assert all training sample exits before month open and no current month trade outcomes enter selection.
- Replay only prequalified 15M-confirmed signal events on the current month, independent changing account equity, native 1M high/low conservatively stop first. Never concatenate monthly or shadow trade ledgers to fake an account. Record 22 monthly net return/PnL, PF, win rate, fees/GST/slippage, trade-close drawdown, by strategy/regime, and 2025/2026.
- Audit invariant: fully closed hour/4H, no label repaint at 3 prefixes, valid 15M confirm next hour, entry after confirmation, global one-position rule, no date-crossing month, ledger PnL+cost cash reconciliation.
- All 22 complete months count: any no-trade month fails target of **22/22 profitable**. 2026 history has been extensively researched, so chronological walk-forward is NOT untouched independent holdout. No cherry-picked live approval on old data, even if a model shows a positive net sample.
- Output a new research branch and report only. Do not modify the live bot or main branch.

**Acceptance:** pass causal/replay integrity checks and measure whether A reduces loss without eliminating trading. Profitability criterion is separate: 22/22 months positive after costs (still not sufficient without independent prospective verification). Honest FAIL acceptable.
