# Reclaim: REDUCE losing months without harming existing profitable months

**Hard rule:** each month historically profitable under the baseline must remain **strictly net positive**, individually for BOTH 1m price path models. A second harder rule preserves the USD profit AMOUNT of each originally profitable month. A profitable month CANNOT become flat.

Baseline: 2% initial SL, 2%->3%->5% profit lock, 1H EMA20 Reclaim, volume ratio >=1.25x, 1m fills, 0.001 BTC, fees and modeled slippage.

Distinct causal filters tested: **6768**; status-preserving AND fewer losing months: **330**; amount-preserving AND fewer losing months: **62**.
