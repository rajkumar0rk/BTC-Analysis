# NOVELTY_CHECK — intrahour path efficiency continuation
Decision: PARTIAL OVERLAP — approved differential, subject to falsification.
Economic hypothesis: a completed 60-minute directional move with unusually low intrahour path length (few reversals) may represent persistent informed execution, leading to short-lived continuation. This is distinct from hourly close-to-close return autocorrelation (research/btc-eth-hourly-return-autocorr-20261118), directional entropy (research/btc-eth-hourly-directional-entropy-20261118), hourly momentum/inside bars (analysis/independent_stage25_hourly_trend), and impact per dollar reversal (analysis/daily_impact_dollar_reversal_20261120_v1). Unlike those studies, the entry variable is the dimensionless ratio of hourly net log displacement to SUM of absolute within-hour minute log returns, ranked against 168 preceding completed hours. Do not claim novelty if an exact matching path-ratio experiment is subsequently found.
Rules: Delta India genuine 1m OHLCV; 60-minute UTC bars only when 60 minutes present. Intrahour path length uses 59 close-to-close minute returns, excluding the hour-boundary return. At completed hour, efficiency > 90th percentile of previous 168 complete hours, abs(log(close/open)) >=0.4%. Enter next minute OPEN in SAME direction. One position, 10% of current NAV notional, no leverage. Stop 0.6%, target 1.2%, timeout 180 minutes; 1m stop-first. Taker 0.05% per side plus 18% GST, adverse BTC >=5 price points per side and ETH >=0.02% per side; double-slip stress. BTC 2025 selection, freeze without tuning before BTC 2026 and ETH. Data-gap signals rejected and counted, not interpolated. Funding, spread, volume units, liquidation and lot rules unavailable: label modeled/inconclusive, never live-proven.
Falsifier: BTC 2025 net expectancy <=0, PF <=1, insufficient independent trades, concentration or unacceptable drawdown; also test stressed costs and frozen 2026/ETH. Daily account NAV marks open trades, missing days explicitly excluded. Missing first IST boundary and October 2026 data preclude claims of complete 22-month coverage. Never optimize using 2026 or ETH.
