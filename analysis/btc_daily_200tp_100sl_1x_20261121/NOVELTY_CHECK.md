# Pre-run novelty audit — 1× BTC futures daily 200 TP / 100 SL, 1H trend + 15M pullback breakout

**Date:** 2026-11-21. **Decision: PARTIAL OVERLAP — approved incremental economic question, NOT a new signal family.**

## Exact deterministic experiment (one fixed configuration; no optimization)

Instrument: Delta Exchange India BTCUSD perpetual, true archive 1-minute OHLCV. Use **complete 2025-01-02 through 2026-10-30 IST days**; Jan 1 2025 and Oct 31 2026 lack a whole IST day in the available 2025-01-01 00:00 UTC–2026-10-30 23:59 UTC archive; log missing bars and reject incomplete dates. Seven days/week, no forced entry, maximum **one opened trade per IST date** and one outstanding position.

**Direction and setup:** 1H trend based on last closed hourly candle: EMA20 > EMA50 and last hourly close > EMA20 for LONG, reversed for SHORT. On a closed 15M candle, last 4 *prior* completed 15M bars contained a genuine touch of their 15M EMA20 (for LONG, low <= that bar EMA20; SHORT, high >= bar EMA20). Current 15M signal candle is bullish and closes above its own EMA20 **and above the immediately preceding 15M candle's high** for LONG; short is exact reversal (bearish, below own EMA20, and below immediately previous 15M low). All 15M 4-bar lookbacks must consist of complete 15M candles, and 1H trend must have >=50 completed hourly closes. The 1H close and EMA are aligned to **last hour ending at or before signal's 15M close** (no incomplete hourly candle). No volume filter, RSI filter, ADX or time-of-day selection. First qualifying entry per IST day only. Enter on exactly the following 1-minute candle's OPEN (when 15m bar closes). No future information.

**Exits:** fixed **BTC chart-price-point TP 200** and **SL 100** from next-minute **raw entry OPEN**, not points net after fees. LONG target raw open+200 and stop raw open−100; SHORT target raw open−200 and stop raw open+100. Each minute, including entry minute, check STOP before target when both touched (conservative). Stop-order gap uses worse of stop and 1m open (same for short); TP fill at target even if gap favorable. If neither is reached, exit at last completed 1m close by **240 minutes max** or the final 1m bar of entry IST day, whichever is first. Never hold across midnight; no overlapping trades. No compounding intra-trade.

**Sizing:** 1× notional exposure = full available account equity divided by raw entry BTCUSD price, **initial equity $10,000**. Positive or negative PnL updates next-trade equity. Costs: taker fee 0.05%/side, 18% GST on each fee, and $5 BTC price adverse slippage **each way**. Fee on executed entry+exit prices. Slippage modeled as exactly 5 BTC points per side. Gross = direction × qty × (exit chart price − entry chart price); account net = gross − qty×10-point roundtrip slippage − both-side fee incl GST. One trade / IST day does *not* guarantee positive daily PnL. Trade close is assigned to entry IST day (always same day), daily zero days count as no trade. For leverage 1× and small equity, real contract lot restrictions not simulated.

**Reports:** CSV of every trade, every IST day, month-by-month actual compounded **NET account** PnL; TP vs SL vs time exit, win rate, positive/loss/no-trade days, months positive/negative/zero, average gross and net BTC-price-point equivalents, profit factor, after-fee P&L, max closed-trade peak-to-trough drawdown, 2025 and Jan–Oct2026 period split. Do not mark 2026 as untouched holdout: it's been inspected in prior BTC-Analysis work. Oct 2026 incomplete despite last full IST day Oct 30. Monthly counts must explicitly say incomplete archive boundaries. No funding, true bid-ask, order book, maker fill or liquidation modeled.
