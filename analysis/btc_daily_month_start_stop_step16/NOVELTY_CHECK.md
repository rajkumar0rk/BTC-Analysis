# Step 16 — Month-start stop-width experiment (all 477 BTC weekday entries retained)

**Novelty gate: PARTIAL OVERLAP, valid incremental exit-risk test.** Earlier studies extensively tested static stop widths and monthly equity-triggered exits (Steps 1/3/7/13–15). This experiment asks whether **narrowing the existing 1-minute protective stop only during the first few complete IST weekdays of each month** can reduce large early-month losing trades, while retaining existing event/fallback direction, full daily exposure and Step13/Step15 exit conditions. A higher 22-month win rate selected after observing 2026 cannot be claimed forward-validated.

## Frozen historical benchmark and caution
- **Step13 (2025-selected baseline):** exactly one BTCUSD futures trade on each of the 477 complete IST Monday–Friday dates 2025-01-02..2026-10-30, 290 positive days, **19/22 winning months**, +23.261256% compounded net account return, 3.008746% closed DD under hypothetical preactivated Scalper Offer with GST and 5 BTCUSD price points adverse slippage per leg.
- **Step15 candidates A and B are FULL-SAMPLE HINDSIGHT references ONLY:** A = 20/22 months, +24.648136%, 2.715507% DD, 292 winning days; B = 21/22 months, +22.480405%, 2.715507% DD, 293 winning days. These were identified after inspecting 2026, and failed precommitted 2025 qualifying conditions. B's only loss was Jan2025 −1.450594%, driven partly by fallback stops ~−0.57%; taking fewer/slimmer positions is NOT permitted as a solution.
- Frozen November 2–20, 2026 has already been researched: both A and B returned −0.396415% across all 15 weekdays, versus Step13 −0.47076%, using hypothetical fee waiver; this cannot be an independent holdout.
- No change to existing hourly 18/19/20 IST first completed 5m volume>=1.5×20 5m prior volume, mandatory 21:00 IST fallback when absent. Entry side fixed from Step11/Step13 causal previous-day arm learner. Entry at 1m OPEN. Every day must enter 1× account notional at original time, exact one live trade. No Sat/Sun, no position sizing or trade-skipping.

## Fixed SL alteration matrix, predeclared before first run
- Each original event trade uses mid SL0.50%; each fallback uses mid SL0.50% or SL0.75% depending on completed prior 5m volume >=1.25×. Trailing and lock remain unchanged: event +0.25% favorable activates +0.10% lock next completed 1m; fallback +0.35% activates 0.20%-distance trailing next completed 1m; TP3% gross; timeout30m; stop-first on 1m collision, stop gap-aware; extra close from PREVIOUS 1m CLOSED candle exits NEXT 1m OPEN only.
- On condition, replace ONLY initial SL percentage with `min(original_SL, cap)` for caps **0.15%,0.20%,0.25%,0.30%,0.35%,0.40%,0.50%**. No stop ever widens or sizes down. Existing trailing/lock/TP/timeout and Step13/A/B early-exit rules are not switched or retuned.
- Month-start activation: one of **first 2, first 3, first 5, or first 8 completed eligible IST weekday trades** in each month. A model may apply this cap on `fallback only` or `event + fallback`. This condition is known at entry (current month ordinal day number), not after observing results.
- Three fixed BASE exit policies evaluated separately, with no 2026-driven family selection: **Step13 unchanged (official prior 2025-selected)**, **Step15 A (hindsight reference)**, **Step15 B (hindsight reference)**. Baseline each family with no cap is also included as regression.
- **3 base policies × 4 month-start windows × 2 scopes × 7 fixed caps = 168 unique tested overlays**, plus three family baselines, each repriced under BOTH fee assumptions. No optional changes after looking at 2026; no picking January-specific dates. Actual trade outcomes update month-to-date MTD for the next trading day only, and each family chooses its existing Step13 / A / B early exit using that model's earlier month returns. Modified stop cap is pre-entry and constant throughout the actual trade.
- 2025-only model selection **within each family**: require >=11/12 profitable full 2025 months, 2025 net return no worse than its family baseline 2025, 2025 winning days no worse than its family baseline, 2025 DD no worse than the family baseline (1e−4 allowance). Rank by number of 2025 winning months, then 2025 compounded net, then 2025 win days, then DD. If no qualifying overlay, retain family baseline. Candidate B/A prior family selection used 2026 hindsight and cannot be treated as 2026 blind validation even if the stop cap is 2025 selected.
- After freezing using 2025 only, apply to Jan–Oct2026 and report unchanged settings: counts of winning months separately 2025 and 2026, all 22 month returns, total 477 days, 1× gross-account exposure, net after fee/GST/slippage and standard taker sensitivity, worst month/day, max DD, profit factor and winning days. An historical monthly improvement means 22/22 profitable months without lowering total net vs its baseline nor increasing DD; otherwise report tradeoffs.
- Publication: exact per-trade parity of all three family baselines with previous Step13 and Step15 runs; output ALL models and family-selected full 477-trade ledgers and 22-month tables; separate full-period `22/22` hindsight-only candidates are NEVER selected using 2026.
- Historical data Jan2025 through Oct2026 have been researched exhaustively. One in-sample 22/22 result cannot validate live monthly profit, and zero loss months going forward is not guaranteed. Historical actual Scalper Offer activation, live bid/ask spread, orderbook, latency, funding, liquidation and intrabar tick ordering cannot be established from OHLCV. Standard fees include 0.05% taker each side +18% GST, and the hypothetical previously activated <=30m Scalper Offer waives only closing fee. Adverse 5 BTCUSD quote points per side.

## Differentiator over related strategies
Unlike universal fixed SL sweeps in Step1 or MTD event/fallback exit variants in Step13–15, this changes stop width only at an **ex ante defined early-month time**, while preserving the higher-consistency frozen exits and requiring exactly one full-risk trade every weekday. Negative or failing performance must be disclosed.
