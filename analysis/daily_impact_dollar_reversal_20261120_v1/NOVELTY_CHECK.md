# Novelty and preregistration — impact-per-dollar liquidity shock reversal
Decision: PARTIAL OVERLAP — approved differential, pending executed falsification.
Prior related families: analysis/institutional_discovery_stage7_time_volume/ (time/volume), research/daily-volume-pressure-20261119, research/daily-jump-aftershock-btcfirst-20261119-1144, research/daily-volatility-shock-fade-20261119-v1. This test differs by conditioning directional price displacement on contemporaneous **notional turnover**, not raw volume, return magnitude alone, candle wick or timing. The hypothesis is that a large directional move achieved with unusually little dollar turnover reflects temporary thin-liquidity impact that partially reverts; this is an OHLCV proxy, NOT historical executable order-book imbalance.
Data: Delta India BTCUSD and ETHUSD 1m monthly CSV.GZ with time,open,high,low,close,volume. If volume absent, missing or inconsistent, FAIL CLOSED. All timestamps UTC; 2025 discovery only, 2026 historical check, ETH unchanged validation. First 2025 IST day and October 2026 gaps must be separately disclosed. Do not infer funding or maker fills.
Fixed baseline: aggregate completed 1m bars into 60-minute UTC bars; impact=abs(log(hour close/hour open))/max(sum(close*volume),epsilon); compare against previous 168 completed hourly impacts (no current-bar leakage). Enter opposite the completed hour's direction at next minute OPEN only if impact > previous 168-hour 95th percentile, absolute hour move >=0.30%, and hourly turnover below its previous 168-hour median. No overlap. Stop 0.60%, take profit 1.00%, maximum 180 minutes, stop-first within each 1m bar. 10% account notional, no leverage; 0.05% fee per side +18% GST, and >=5 BTC points slippage per side; ETH >=0.02% per side. Stress double slippage. No parameter tuning on 2026/ETH. Exit on gap at next available open only if contiguous minutes; otherwise mark INCONCLUSIVE, never skip losses.
Falsifier: nonpositive BTC 2025 after-cost trade expectancy or PF<=1, inadequate sample, destructive drawdown, or failure under stress. A passing 2025 result still needs frozen 2026 and ETH and execution/funding validation.
This is a novel incremental causal measurement versus prior raw volume-pressure and jump studies; if the exact same impact/turnover conjunction is found in prior code, mark DUPLICATE and cancel.
