## Source execution defects requiring explicit, separate comparison
1. Entry signal on 4H bar i opens position at open[i+1] immediately in the loop for bar i. Next iteration processes bar i+1 with that position, so fill is temporally inconsistent with accounting at bar i and fees are absent.
2. On each bar, stop is checked BEFORE pyramiding. An intrabar add can occur on a bar whose low already passed the newly raised common stop. OHLC alone cannot establish high-before-low; faithful reproduction and conservative stop-first correction must be distinguished.
3. Intrabar high-trigger additions at exact trigger are assumed filled even when the bar gaps above it. Conservative gap-through execution must use open if worse; same for stops, which currently fill at ideal levels even on adverse opening gaps.
4. The hard stop is evaluated from candle low, but it is checked before common ATR stop; if both touched, fill ordering requires conservative treatment. The original does not deduct commissions/GST, slippage, spread, funding, or margin liquidation.
5. Original 20x cap is not permission to use extreme leverage; each unit's 2% risk and up to 4 units require separate margin feasibility. It calculates leverage using realized account balance rather than marked-to-market equity.
6. Trade NAV is recorded only on closes, with no intratrade equity or IST month-end mark-to-market. No 22-month monthly table, no funding, and no transaction costs. Published performance cannot be considered Delta India evidence.
7. At end it force-closes on last close; daily EMA is aligned from shifted daily candle and must be rebuilt with completed UTC days, not current unfinished day.
8. Original source is long-only; a short counterpart is NOT part of the original strategy and must not be silently introduced.
