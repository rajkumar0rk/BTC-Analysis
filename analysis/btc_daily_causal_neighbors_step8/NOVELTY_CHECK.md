## Hard, frozen daily specification
- Authentic Delta India BTCUSD 1m uninterrupted archive Jan2025–Oct2026, exactly **477 full IST Monday–Friday days** between 2025-01-02 and 2026-10-30; one and ONLY one BTC futures position every day, zero skipped dates, no weekends.
- Entry *times unchanged*: at the first of 18/19/20 IST hours when the last complete 5m volume >=1.5× prior 20 complete 5m averages, else mandatory fallback. Event direction candidates = FADE prior 5m or FOLLOW prior 5m. Fallback choices: 21:00 or 22:00 IST × FOLLOW/FADE prior 5m or 1h. If a 22h fallback arm is chosen, the arm-selection decision is made BEFORE 21:00 using only features at 21h; the directional sign of 22h arm is computed at 22h prior to execution. Never choose hour by seeing the later 22h movement.
- Event exit unchanged from Step6: 1m stop-first SL0.5% mid, gross TP3%, maxhold30m, profit lock +0.10% after +0.25% favorable move valid next complete candle. Fallback exit remains E: trailing activation +0.35%, distance0.20%, SL0.5% or 0.75% depending on its own last completed 5m preentry volume ratio >=1.25; TP3%, maxhold30m. 5 BTC USD price-points adverse slippage EACH leg. Taker 0.05% each leg +18% GST; hypothetical preactivated regular Scalper Offer waives only closing fee/GST if within 30m. Exactly 1× account notional, no leverage/funding/orderbook, and no authenticated historical offer eligibility.
- Baseline Step6 online `online_impulse5` event choice using prior30 relevant event days, min12 prior comparable, 0.0003 mean-net improvement margin; fallback 21h FOLLOW5; assert **per-trade and overall parity** against Step6 under offer.

## Genuinely different causal direction learner
- At each day BEFORE entry, compute fixed-scale features from **only completed 1m bars** (last 5/15/60/240m % returns, 5m volume ratio, 1h range position and range percent, recent 15m range expansion, alignment, hour), with no normalization fitted on future history or any future prices.
- For event, fix the direction-neutral reference as FADE5; for fallback, reference at 21 IST as FOLLOW5. Features encoded relative to reference sign to generalize trend versus fade.
- For the day to be traded, find k nearest **PAST** similar events/fallbacks using two preregistered fixed-scale feature maps: `compact` (relative 5m/15m/60m returns, volume ratio, 1h range position), `extended` (+240m return, last15m range ratio, hour, 60m range percent). Neighbor pool is only the previous 45/90/180 same-entry-type weekdays; k=12/24/36 neighbors (take fewer if pool shorter, require >=12). Distance Euclidean over fixed scaled/clipped features, **never refit scaling with future data**.
- Evaluate 2 arms (event) and 4/8 arms (fallback; 21-only or 21/22) on those previous closed weekdays using shadow outcomes already determined at each prior day’s OHLC. Choose based on distance-weighted **mean net after-cost return**, **win fraction**, or **mean net − 0.3× downside deviation**. These scores are not labels from the current day.
- Baseline fallback = 21h FOLLOW5. Choose non-default arm only if score improves over default by more than 0 or (conservative) at least 0.0003 net fraction / 0.05 winning-day fraction. If <12 prior same-type days: use baseline defaults; no skipped trades.
- Two event choice modes: unchanged Step6 online_impulse5 or new nearest-neighbor. Two fallback choice modes: unchanged 21h FOLLOW5 or new nearest-neighbor; two fallback arm spaces (only 21h, or 21h/22h).
- Frozen grid = 2 feature maps ×3 windows ×3 k ×3 scoring ×2 margins ×2 event-choice modes ×2 fallback-choice modes ×2 fallback-arm spaces = **864 policies**. Evaluate **same 864 actual chosen paths** under both modeled fee schedules. Do not use standard-fee results to re-select signals; online selection scores are calculated under the assumed Scalper Offer, and standard fees are a sensitivity reprice of the SAME trades. Include Step6 baseline.
- 2025-only selection: among policies earning >=+3% net in 2025 with >=145 winning days and <=4% max closed DD in 2025, rank by 2025 winning-day count, 2025 net return, 2025 drawdown. If none, no candidate beats baseline. Freeze selected policy and report 2026 Jan–Oct **without tuning**, plus total winning days, net compound, 22 months, worst month/day, streak, drawdown. Explicitly test whether selected version improves BOTH 2025 and 2026 winning-day count and net returns relative to Step6, without raising DD.
- Full-period best-of-864 is HINDSIGHT ONLY and not a deployable strategy. November 2026 has been examined already; do not use it as training here. Future forward paper trading is mandatory before real deployment.
- No skipping weekdays, shrinking trade count, changing TP to +0.2%, adding leverage, using current-day future candle labels in online selection, or selecting hours after observing later prices.

## Limitations
Tick-level sequencing unavailable: assume stop before TP on 1m collision; trailing update only next minute. Scalper offer has not been historically authenticated. 2026 has been intensively research-exposed and is not pristine blind validation. Large prior research history introduces data snooping despite causal per-day selection.
