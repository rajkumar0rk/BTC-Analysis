# Novelty assessment — hourly range occupancy
Decision: PARTIAL OVERLAP — approved differential for testing, not a proven edge.

Economic mechanism: price may dwell near one side of the hourly range as participants absorb supply/demand; repeated acceptance may predict continuation, while exhausted occupancy may predict reversal. OHLCV cannot establish actual passive order absorption. Falsifier: after-cost BTC 2025 matched baseline and month distribution show no material advantage, or frozen BTC 2026 collapses.

Distinction: this uses the fraction of 60 completed minute CLOSES in the top versus bottom 20% of their completed hour's high-low range, not last close location, runs, or volume correlations. No future-minute signal at entry.

Rules: Completed 60-minute UTC hour only. Compute fraction of closes in top 20% and bottom 20%; if abs(difference)>=0.30 or 0.45, enter next minute open in direction of dominant extreme (continuation) or opposite (reversal).
