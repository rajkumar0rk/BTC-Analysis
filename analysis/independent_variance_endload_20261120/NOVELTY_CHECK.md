# Novelty and preregistration — intrahour variance end-loading
Decision: PARTIAL OVERLAP — approved differential for falsification, not presumed edge.
Closest read-only prior: research/independent-hourly-volume-backload-20261119-v1 (volume concentration), research/independent-variance-term-structure-20261119-v1 (multi-hour variance), research/daily-jump-aftershock-btcfirst-20261119-1144 (jump follow-through). This study tests the *timing of squared one-minute returns within a single complete hour*, not volume, hourly variance ratio, or jump size.
Hypothesis: an abrupt late-hour shift in realized price variance after an unusually quiet first 45 minutes signals a new information regime; continuation can create right-tail futures returns, despite frequent small stops.
Predeclared rules: complete 60-minute bars only; squared log-return sum in final 15 minutes > 4x sum in first 45 minutes, last 15-minute absolute return >0.30%, and close direction agrees with last 15-minute move; enter at following one-minute open, never at signal candle close. One position at a time. Initial stop 0.65% of entry; 0.25% of current NAV risk, capped 2x notional; after 4R favorable excursion trail 2R behind best observed minute extreme, stop-first each minute, exit after 48h. No selection on ETH/2026. BTC 2025 discovery, BTC Jan-Oct 2026 exposed robustness; identical dimensionless rules on ETH. Control: reversed direction with same risk and entry timing. Fixed 0.05% per-side taker fee plus 18% GST, adverse slippage max(5 BTC USD, 0.02% price) per side for BTC, 0.02% for ETH; stressed 2x slippage. No invented funding. Data SHA and gaps recorded. Monthly marked-to-market NAV including open positions; monthly realized after-cost PF separately. No annual claim if missing data.
Falsifier: no recurring after-cost BTC positive expectancy / PF >=1.5, no >=10R realized winner, excessive drawdown, or sensitivity to cost; if rejected, do not retune.
Known coverage limitations: January 1 2025 IST pre-UTC-year boundary and October 2026 gap must be checked; report partial months explicitly.
