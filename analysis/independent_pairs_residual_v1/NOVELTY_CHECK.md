# Novelty check — ETH/BTC two-leg residual mean reversion

Decision: **PARTIAL OVERLAP — approved differential**. This experiment is NOT a renamed directional cross-asset confirmation, dispersion filter, or directional engulfing/breakout. It trades a **simultaneous dollar-neutral ETH/BTC spread** when the lagged rolling log ETH/BTC price ratio deviates from its lagged historical mean, and exits on spread reversion, residual stop, or time limit. BTC and ETH are both executed in every trade; separately report each leg's signed net contribution and the pair's total account P&L. The same price/indicator calculations apply to both markets; paired results cannot honestly be interpreted as two standalone BTC-only and ETH-only strategies.

Closest prior references: codex/STRATEGY_NOVELTY_GATE.md; scripts/research_btc_eth_profit_factor_pairs.py (single-instrument paired family baseline); research/independent-crossasset-dispersion-pf-20261118 (cross-asset volatility-normalized *directional filter*); analysis/institutional_carry_stage1 (funding carry, not residual spot-price spread). Other crossasset research may overlap; classify as differential rather than wholly novel.

Rules: 1h fully closed candles; log(ETH/BTC) rolling mean/std using previous 120, 240 or 480 hours, including latest closed hour. Enter both legs at the NEXT hour's first 1m open if z > 1.5/2/2.5 (short ETH long BTC) or z < negative threshold (long ETH short BTC); one pair at a time. Close on 1m price-ratio mean crossing, residual distance 1.5/2 times entry residual std beyond entry, or 24/48h timeout. Both legs closed at IST month-end to make actual realized monthly account returns; same execution latency and cost model for both. Risk at most 0.5% equity per estimated residual stop, max 2x gross notional. BTC 5 absolute points adverse per fill; ETH 0.02% per fill; 0.05% taker + 18% GST per side on EACH leg. Stop checked on 1m close (intraminute stop breaches are not captured; risk limitation). Historical execution prices unknown; no order-book depth, funding, margin/liquidation or fill impact. No leverage scaling to force returns.

2025 is used for selection only; 2026 Jan–Oct historical check is already research-exposed, not virgin forward. Jan 2025 IST first 5.5 hours unavailable. Both original archives verified by per-file SHA256 in loader; Oct 2026 manifest is validated before run. No live orders, no main merge.
