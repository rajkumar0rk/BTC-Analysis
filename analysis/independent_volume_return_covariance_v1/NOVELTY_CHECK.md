# NOVELTY CHECK — hourly return-volume covariance
Decision: NEW observable conditional-participation statistic; provisional, not proof of order flow.
Mechanism: unusually strong correlation between minute returns and minute traded volume within a completed hour may reflect price changes concentrated in high-participation minutes. Such a pattern could represent temporary urgency followed by reversal or continuation; both are falsifiable. Candle volume cannot identify aggressor side, liquidations or institutional actors.
Closest comparisons: analysis/independent_volume_impact_residual_v1/ (magnitude of hourly return normalized by total volume); analysis/independent_hourly_volume_backload_v1/ (late-hour volume concentration); analysis/independent_vwap_twap_dislocation_v1/ (difference between price averages). This statistic measures signed minute-return/volume covariance, not total volume, timing concentration, or price-level weighting. Distinction is economic and computational, though signal overlap remains unmeasured.
Rules: aggregate 60 completed 1m bars to each hour; Pearson correlation of minute log returns and minute volumes (first minute's return omitted when crossing hour boundary). If absolute correlation >=0.20 or >=0.35, enter next minute open in direction of hourly close-to-close return or opposite. 14h ATR stop 1.5x, 2R take profit, 12h timeout; 1m stop-first/gap-aware exits; 0.5% NAV stop risk, 3x notional cap, one position at a time. Choose between the four predeclared configurations using BTC 2025 net NAV return only; then freeze for BTC 2026 and ETH 2025–Oct 2026. BTC slip 5 USD per side; ETH 0.02% per side; taker fee 0.05% each side +18% GST. Source partition SHA validation. Funding, real spreads, market impact, margin/liquidation, lot rounding unmodeled. First 5.5 IST hours Jan 2025 unavailable. 2026 research-exposed.
Falsifier: after-cost BTC monthly NAV and PF fail recurring material profitability or costs destroy the effect. No retroactive 2026/ETH tuning.
