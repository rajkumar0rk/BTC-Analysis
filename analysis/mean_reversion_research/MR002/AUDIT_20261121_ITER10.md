## IMPORTANT: completeness limitation on the 22 IST months

The earliest source candle is 2025-01-01 **00:00 UTC**, which equals **2025-01-01 05:30 IST**. Thus the first 5h30m of January 2025 IST (2024-12-31 18:30–23:59 UTC) are missing. The first month is **partial** even though the original script labels it as a full IST month. Its observed negative return is genuine for the available period but **cannot be called a complete January IST result**. All subsequent months through October 2026 have their required UTC window in the archive (subject to raw timestamp semantics verification). This limits verified *complete* IST months to 21, not 22. The 6/22 and 16/22 are descriptive for the archived monthly table, not 22 fully observed calendar months. A future rerun should either source 2024-12-31 18:30–23:59 UTC or explicitly mark January partial; never fill with invented candles.
