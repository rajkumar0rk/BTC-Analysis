# Step 23 — Intraday historical-bar supervised direction predictor, including first January

**Novelty: partial overlap but materially different training sample.** Step22 Ridge/logistic model used previous **same-type DAILY trade** counterfactual outcomes, requiring >=40 previous events/fallbacks; therefore its predictions were ALL disabled in January 2025, making correction of that month logically impossible. 108 Step22 policies kept at most 21/22 profitable months; a hindsight fallback Ridge variant delivered +26.468595% but not 22/22. This experiment keeps the frozen Step20 21-month exit/entry setup and builds a supervised direction model using **previously completed 30-minute BTC candle forward returns sampled every 30 minutes** from prior 1–7 calendar days. This can train before the first Jan2 2025 trade using Jan1 2025 archive data, without depending on previously completed daily strategy positions. No current/future candle input. Prior static momentum and kNN strategies studied similar features, so not a completely new prediction theory and no expectation of guaranteed edge.

## Frozen model hypothesis and exact 54-policy grid
- Candidate per-minute training sample at 30-minute spaced times t for which at least 300 prior one-minute bars exist, training labels are **future 30-minute raw midprice return** `close[t+29]/open[t]−1`, and `t+30 <= current_entry_i`. No future information relative to the current actual trade timestamp.
- At each current entry timestamp i, train on completed minute-labeled historical samples from **last 1, 3, or 7 calendar days** (3 windows), all available times/hours, selected every 30 minutes. Minimum **24 prior training samples**, else keep original Step11 direction. At Jan2 2025 entries, many Jan1 labeled observations are available, avoiding Step22's cold-start. A label is only used after its forward 30-minute outcome is fully closed.
- Every training example feature is measured solely from candles `[t−N,t)` (prior completed 1m): 5m,15m,60m,240m signed returns (fixed scaling), completed 5m relative volume versus preceding20 nonoverlapping complete 5m bins, prior 60m range fraction and location in range, completed SMA20–SMA60 gap, ratio of 14 prior positive vs negative minute close changes, and prior 15m range. Current trade uses identically computed features from `[i−N,i)`. No day-index, month identity, realized return after i, target future candle or hindsight-specific January logic is used.
- Algorithms: L2 Ridge(alpha=10) linear regression of 30m raw return in basis points / train-label standard deviation, and L2 LogisticRegression(C=.25,solver liblinear) of future30m price positive/negative classification; StandardScaler learned ONLY from the prior train window.
- Confidence to override frozen original side: |linear standardized predicted sign| or |2×logistic p(up)−1| >= **0.20, 0.40, 0.60** (3 fixed values). Scope **volume event only**, **mandatory fallback only**, or **both** (3). If confidence low, leave original. No trade ever skipped.
- **2 algorithms ×3 lookback-day windows ×3 confidence cutoffs ×3 scopes =54 policies plus unchanged baseline** (55 strategies, 110 fee-sensitivity rows); same 477 weekday entries, original exit rules and account-exposure assumption for every case.
- Mandatory baseline daily parity with Step20 B under both costs; all candidate models full 477 weekdays; no future-labeled sample train leakage. Save every candidate's 2025 and 2026 metrics, 22 monthly compounded account returns, max closed drawdown, worst month/day, winning days and fees.
- Selection using **2025 only**: require >=12/12 positive 2025 months, 2025 net >= Step20 B +8.882867%, 2025 positive weekdays>=153, and 2025 DD<=2.617670%; rank by 2025 positive months, 2025 net, 2025 win days, lower DD. If none qualify, select unchanged baseline and candidly report failure. Freeze then check Jan–Oct2026 (ALREADY mined by many prior steps; NOT pristine validation). Genuine improvement at full period additionally requires 22/22 months, net >=+25.423847%, max DD<=2.617670%, no degradation of 2026 monthly count (10/10) and all 477 trades.
- Any full 22-month best identified after observing 2026 must be labeled HINDSIGHT ONLY and NOT used for promotion. Even improved January alone does not justify choice if losses move to another month. Label analysis clearly that training on same historical regime can overfit even when sample labels are causal.
- No realtime live execution, guaranteed profit, leverage change or main branch merge. Real Scalper historical waiver, live spreads, orderbook depth, funding, latency and intraminute stops remain unverified.

## Causal sample definition
At entry i, complete 30m historical samples require t+30<=i. Features at t use only completed candles before t. The label uses close[t+29], which is complete before the entry minute begins. Training data from earlier on the same calendar day are allowed only if that 30m period has already finished before i; never use the current day's 1m stop/TP result to train earlier in that day.
