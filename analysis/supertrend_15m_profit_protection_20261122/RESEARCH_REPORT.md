## Execution caveats
- Protective stops ratchet with 1m highs, and are hit if the 1m low is at or below the ratcheted stop. If target/stop are both reached in the same 1m, count **protective stop first**.
- New arming and protective stop hit in one 1m is **ambiguous**; a pessimistic high-then-low outcome at the newly armed stop is chosen and flagged, but tick data could differ; do not call results live-executable.
- Closing-stop remains next 1m open after 15m bearish/breach close.
- Protected breakeven measured from pre-slippage entry; fee/GST and entry/exit slip mean protected BE is **net negative**.
- One trade per bullish Supertrend cycle; a filtered-out touch may be followed by a later qualifying touch in the same cycle.
- All 2026 history has been research-exposed; no untouched OOS.
