# NOVELTY CHECK — hourly directional sign-run persistence
Decision: NEW, pending empirical validation.
Economic hypothesis: persistent same-direction minute returns may represent price discovery spread across consecutive minutes, whereas rapidly alternating signs may reflect replenishing liquidity. With matched up/down base rates, a low sign-switch ratio should predict subsequent continuation (or reversal if transient pressure exhausts). This is a falsifiable statistical price-path mechanism; OHLCV cannot prove institutional actors or forced order flow.
Closest comparisons: analysis/independent_volume_return_covariance_v1/ tests signed return/volume covariance; analysis/independent_extreme_cluster_relaxation_v1/ examines clustered extreme magnitudes; analysis/independent_variance_term_structure_v1/ compares volatility across timescales; analysis/independent_cusum_regime_shift_v1/ examines structural shifts. Here the signal counts consecutive return-sign transitions conditional on the hour's positive/negative sign balance, not return magnitude, volume, variance, or regime-shift thresholds. Potential conceptual overlap is acknowledged.
Rules: completed 60-minute UTC hour, within-hour nonzero 1m log-return signs; require >=40 comparable adjacent pairs. Sign switch ratio = observed sign-change fraction / (2p(1-p)), where p is within-hour positive-sign frequency. Trigger ratio <=0.65 or <=0.80, enter next minute open in completed-hour return direction or opposite. One position, 14-hour ATR stop 1.5x, 2R target, 12h timeout, stop-first 1m fills, 0.5% NAV stop risk, max 3x notional. BTC 2025 selects 4 variants by after-cost NAV return; freeze for BTC 2026 and native ETH. Fees 0.05% each side plus 18% GST, BTC 5 USD/side slippage, ETH 0.02%/side. Funding, actual spread, market impact, margin/liquidation and lots unavailable. First 5.5h Jan 2025 IST unavailable; 2026 research exposed; BTC October repair must be SHA-verified.
Falsifier: insufficient recurrent after-cost BTC NAV growth and monthly PF, or cost-stressed degradation.
