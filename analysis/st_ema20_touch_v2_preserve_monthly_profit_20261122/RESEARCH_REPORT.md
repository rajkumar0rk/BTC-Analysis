## Stress sensitivity

Additional per-side slippage is applied as a P&L adjustment per trade **without rerunning entry trigger and stop filling**. Therefore this is an approximate sensitivity only; more robust tick/order-book forward testing required.

## Limitations

All results based on the same 2025-26 dataset already used to tune V1; severe selection bias. Positive historical profits are small relative to execution model uncertainty. Does NOT model funding, margin, liquidation, exact tick sequence, order amendment latency, market depth or missing orders. Realized drawdown 0% is not maximum intratrade risk.
