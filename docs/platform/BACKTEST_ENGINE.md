# M3 reference event simulator

Implemented in `btc_analysis.backtest`. Run trusted strategy factories with a pinned config and repeatable candle factory. Strategy callbacks follow the Python SDK. No new visual builder/API or untrusted execution yet. Use synthetic fixtures until a reviewed open-label data attestation exists.

## Execution sequence
For each execution candle: previously submitted orders are eligible at its open, then simulated price path is processed in traversal order. Market orders fill at next open; limit touch assumes full quantity; stop market gaps fill at open with modeled adverse slippage; stop limit triggers then may rest or fill. Tick rounding is adverse for market orders. Fee and tax are explicit. Stop/reduce priority at simultaneous price events; FIFO within priority. At close publish completed execution/signal candles, notify fills, call on_execution and on_signal. Orders submitted by callbacks cannot execute in the already completed bar. on_fill sees final bar state and receives the recorded individual fill. All pending orders expire at the final close. Open positions are marked, not forced closed.

BOTH_PATHS runs full independent O-H-L-C and O-L-H-C scenarios, records each and identifies lower final equity. STOP_FIRST is adverse-first heuristic based on starting position/first pending entry direction, not a proof of worst case. No exact intrabar timestamps or true queue/volume available. Order log, execution evidence and original reason retained. Protective reduce-only orders canceled when position goes flat. Strategy cancellation effective at next bar; no intrabar callbacks. No new same-bar bracket from entry-fill callback. Static resting brackets may be submitted before execution but reduce-only orders without positions are rejected if touched first.

## Accounting
Single linear perpetual, same quote/settlement/account currency, 1x only, one net position; FIFO realized lots, scaling/partial exits; reversal exceeding position rejected, reduce-only clipped. Basic notional plus fees cap at entry. Exact Decimal accounting (50 digits context), signed mark P&L; fees/tax deducted at fill, gross/net and monthly MTM reconciled. No real exchange margin, funding, spread, liquidation, rebates or inverse P&L; unsupported configs fail closed. Maker assumption for intrabar resting limits, taker for marketable opens/triggers. Event drawdown tracks simulated price points, not tick evidence. Equity curve at signal close. Months attributed using execution interval open in reporting timezone. Causal history bounded 4096 bars; result arrays and order counts bounded by explicit quotas, not a high-performance kernel.

## Provenance
Pinned dataset manifest supplied by trusted adapter; each observed candle stream independently SHA-256 hashed and compared across scenarios. Result canonical hash independent of wall clock. Caller responsible for verifying source manifest, linear contract, strategy code/parameter hashes and fresh deterministic strategy instance; Python is not isolated. Engine rejects gaps, invalid identity and incomplete ranges; requires continuous UTC and signal-aligned endpoints. M4 adds optional separate pre-start warmup and declared streaming indicators; see [Python SDK](PYTHON_SDK.md).

## Tests
`python -m unittest discover -s tests/platform -p test_backtest_engine.py -v` exercises chronological fills, multi-timeframe visibility, ambiguity, stops and limits, partial exits, costs, positions, no future access, gap rejection and determinism. CI runs entire platform suite. Real data integration requires verified candle timestamp semantics; legacy research remains unchanged.
