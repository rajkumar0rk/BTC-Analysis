# Reviewed MTF Supertrend + EMA20 Pullback library scripts

Two exact, versioned reviewed Python wrappers are available in Cloud strategy library:
- `strategies/cloud/mtf_supertrend_ema20_touch_100_200.py`
- `strategies/cloud/mtf_supertrend_ema20_reclaim_100_200.py`

Use Strategies → Load Touch/Reclaim → Save cloud version. This creates an owner-scoped immutable strategy only when the signed-in owner clicks Save. Use source in local backtest for short or continuous repository replay. No server-side Python execution, paper-session integration or live orders. The reviewed wrappers import the SDK strategy; byte-identical source is required and edited Python is rejected by the runner.

Semantics follow the user's rules and the retained original research `scripts/research_st_mtf_ema20_fixed_rr.py` (research branch). Confirmed 1H and 15M Supertrend ATR 10 × 3, 15M EMA20. Touch: previous 1m close strictly on trend side, current 1m open on trend side, full range contains previous confirmed EMA, unchanged alignment at close. Reclaim: previous 15m close on trend side, completed 15m candle touches prior EMA and closes back on trend side, alignment at entry. Next minute open market entry, 100h initial in-range warmup, IST Monday–Friday entries, exits all days, one actual entry per contiguous aligned cycle, one net position, no trend-flip exits, no trailing stop. Stop/target fixed 100/200 USD price points from modeled fill, active within entry minute; stop market gaps and slippage can exceed 100 points, limit fills are modeled. Risk-enabled replay may override exits for liquidation/pre-liq. No forced terminal close; mark residual open position. Existing engine models two OHLC paths rather than the original stop-first-only loop; indicator seeding and sizing may differ. Exposure defaults to 50% of equity (retained cloud wrapper assumption), multiplied by selected leverage only for explicit leveraged replay. Fees/GST/slippage/funding follow runner settings. No claim of research P&L parity. Graph-based optimizer does not accept this stateful recipe yet.
